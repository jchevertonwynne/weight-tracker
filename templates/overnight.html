Weigh-in calculator

Enter the weight you need to hit at tomorrow's morning weigh-in. With nothing entered, the boxes below show the overnight change (kg) over each timescale; once you enter a target, they shift onto the bedtime weight that would get you there. Weighing less is always fine — each column runs from your best night on record at the bottom to your worst at the top, so the green end is the weight that every night you have logged would have carried to the target, and your odds fade out from there. There is no line to land on, only a colour that gets worse the higher you go. The numbers down the side of each column are its worst night, its average (in bold) and its best; hover a column for the odds behind them and the number of nights logged.

{{/* Which timescales appear depends on how much history exists — a 90-day window over ten days of data repeats the 30-day one — so /overnight/windows decides the set and chart-app.js draws all of it. */}}

{{/* Only meaningful once a target is entered and the boxes are projected onto bedtime weight; chart-app.js toggles it. */}}
  • At or below — every night on record makes it
  • Fading — fewer of your nights make it, the higher you go
  • Above — no night on record makes it
  • Black line and bold number — your average night, where it is a coin flip
